Co-op mod for White Knuckle

Don't climb alone.

Up to 8 climbers in the same tower, roped together. One mass rising under all of you, doors that wait for the team, revives at the terminals. Leave someone behind and you'll be the one hanging.

Segment 01

What you get

BLY-01

Up to 8 players

Host a public, friends-only or private Steam lobby. Friends join through Steam invites. No port forwarding.

BLY-02

Same tower

Everyone climbs the same levels and gets the same world events and spawns. Enemies hunt whoever is nearest, not just the host.

BLY-03

The rope

An optional climbing rope ties the team together. It swings and catches on ledges. Grab it to climb toward a teammate, or hold V on a wall to haul up whoever is below.

BLY-04

One mass

The mass rises for everyone and chases whoever is lowest. Nobody gets to hang back.

BLY-05

Doors wait

A door that shuts behind you stays open until every living teammate is through. The elevator only leaves once the whole team is aboard.

BLY-06

Revives

Dead players spectate. A teammate brings them back at a terminal for a few roaches, or for free with a disk they're carrying.

BLY-07

Your own wallet

Roaches are never shared. What you pick up is yours, and you pay for your own revives.

BLY-08

Team votes

Shortcuts need a vote at the shortcut vote terminal. The losing path stays shut, so nobody splits the team.

BLY-09

Updates itself

New versions download when the game starts and apply after a restart. The Belay menu also turns your other mods on and off.

Install

  1. Close White KnuckleThen download the installer zip with the button at the top.

  2. Extract the whole zipAnywhere you like (right-click, Extract All). Running it from inside the zip won't work.

  3. Run "Install Belay.bat"It finds your game through Steam and installs BepInEx (the mod loader) and Belay. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway.

  4. Start the gameClick "Belay" in the bottom right of the main menu, or press F7. Host a lobby or join a friend's. Everyone needs the same Belay version.

F7 Belay menu
V haul up teammate (rope on)
Tab switch who you spectate

Already run BepInEx 5? Grab the mod-only zip and put BepInEx\plugins\Belay\Belay.dll in your game folder. To remove Belay, run "Uninstall Belay.bat". It leaves BepInEx and your other mods alone.

FAQ

Does everyone need to own White Knuckle?

Yes. Belay is a mod for the Steam version of the game. It doesn't include or unlock the game.

Is it safe? Windows warned me about the installer.

The warning appears for any new download that isn't code-signed. The installer is a plain script you can open in Notepad. It only touches your White Knuckle folder.

Will it break my saves?

Solo play is unchanged. In a co-op lobby, runs use the host's progression and disk saves are switched off so the team can't desync.

I have another multiplayer mod installed.

Switch it off in the Belay menu (F7, Mods tab) and restart the game. Two multiplayer mods at once will fight each other.

Something broke. Where do I report it?

Open an issue on GitHub and attach BepInEx\LogOutput.log from your game folder.
